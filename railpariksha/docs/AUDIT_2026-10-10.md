## Checked and fine
- **Website** (live): 500 random pages of 5,434 all return 200; no duplicate titles or descriptions; every page has one H1, a canonical, hreflang for Hindi, English and x-default, a viewport and valid JSON-LD; sitemap 3 MB, robots, `app-ads.txt` (AdMob publisher line) present. Lighthouse (mobile): performance 89 to 97, accessibility, best practices and SEO 100.
- **App**: `flutter analyze` no issues; all 1,381 tests pass; real AdMob unit IDs are used only by the release build (`USE_TEST_ADS=false`), test IDs everywhere else; content feed and the PYQ pack release (121 files) are reachable; the daily current-affairs workflow succeeds every day; release builds succeeded on 8 Oct.
- **YouTube**: every scheduled and manual upload so far returned a Shorts URL; neural voice works with no fallback; titles lead with exam, subject and year; descriptions carry the links and five hashtags.
- **Telegram**: 120 days of hourly quizzes (2,160 polls) and 120 days of Shorts (720) are within every length limit, never run out of questions and never repeat in a language (one pair of different questions has identical text); tip, fact and weekly texts are far under the 4,096 limit.

- Long Search titles on PYQ pages (about half are over 65 characters) are cut by Google after the exam name; the subject and exam come first, so they still read well.
- The scheduled workflows rely on the repository's default branch name (`aniketai/relaxed-albattani-6kjmc7`) in the Worker variable `REF`.
