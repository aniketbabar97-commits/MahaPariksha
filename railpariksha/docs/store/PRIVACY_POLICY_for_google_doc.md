# RailPariksha — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [fill in today's date when you publish this]_

RailPariksha does not collect personal data. There is no sign-up or account creation.

**Your progress is stored only on your device.** Your answers, streaks, XP, Beast Mode scores, and settings are saved locally on your phone and are deleted automatically when you uninstall the app or use "Reset progress" in Settings.

**Internet use.** The app connects to the internet only to download updated question packs. In this version, no analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data are collected.

**Feedback and reporting.** If you choose to report a question or send feedback, your device's email app opens and you decide what to send to us. We do not automatically receive any information from you.

**Ads (when enabled).** If and when this app shows ads, it will use Google AdMob, which may collect an advertising identifier and usage data to show relevant ads, in line with Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). This section will be updated with specifics before ads go live, and the "Ads" declaration in this app's Play Store listing will reflect that change.

**Third parties.** RailPariksha is an independent educational app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Indian Railways, the Railway Recruitment Board (RRB), the Railway Protection Force (RPF), or any official recruitment body. All exam names are used only to describe what the app helps you study for.

**Children.** This app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

**Changes to this policy.** If this policy changes (for example, when ads or an account system are added), the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be posted at this same link.
