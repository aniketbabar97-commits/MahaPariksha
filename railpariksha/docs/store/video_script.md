# Play Store promo video script — RailPariksha

Google Play lets you attach a short video (YouTube link) to the listing; apps with
one consistently convert better than listing screenshots alone, especially for an
exam-prep category where "does this actually look good" is the #1 install hesitation.
This is a script, not a video — I can't shoot footage myself, but this is written so
you (or a ₹500-1500 Fiverr/Upwork video editor) can screen-record the real app exactly
as described and have a finished 30-second video same-day. No actors, voice talent,
or animation needed — every shot is a real screen recording of the app.

**Length: 30 seconds** (Play's sweet spot — long enough to show value, short enough
nobody skips). **Format: 9:16 vertical** (plays natively everywhere: Play listing,
Instagram/YouTube Shorts reuse later). **Music: any upbeat, royalty-free Hindi/Indian-
feel track** under ₹500 on a stock site (Pixabay Music is free and license-clean).

## Practical recording notes

- Record each shot on a real phone (screen-record, not emulator — emulator frame
  rate looks choppy in exported video). Android's built-in screen recorder
  (Settings → quick-settings tile) is fine.
- Pre-seed demo data before recording shot 6 (Beast Mode) and shot 7 (leaderboard) so
  the streak/rank numbers look good on camera — don't record a 0-streak, 0-rank cold
  start.
- Shot 2's on-screen text and shot 8's final card are the only text overlays that need
  real design work (5 minutes in Canva, using the brand colors from `theme.dart`:
  sky blue `#0B3D91` + saffron `#F5B400`). Every other overlay is optional — the app's
  own UI text already says enough.
- Export at 1080×1920, H.264, under 30MB so it uploads cleanly to YouTube (required —
  Play Console takes a YouTube link, not a direct file upload).
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** (not Private — Play's crawler needs to reach it,
  but Unlisted keeps it out of search/recommendations and your public channel if any).
