## How the Shorts are worded (done by shorts.py)
- Title: exam + subject + year first, for example "RRB Group D CBT Maths PYQ 2025: can you solve it?" (that is what students search). No hashtags in the title.
- Description: the keyword line first (the part search and the feed read), then the app, website, Telegram and YouTube links, then five hashtags (the first three show above the title). YouTube ignores hashtags past 15, so we stay well under.
- Tags: the exam, "<exam> previous year questions", subject and a few Hindi phrases; tags have little weight, the title and first description lines have the most.
- What moves the feed most is behaviour: viewers who watch to the end, replays, likes, comments and shares. The countdown and the answer reveal are there for that.
