# RUKHSA Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RUKHSA ("**RUKHSA**", "**we**", "**us**", or
"**our**") handles information in connection with the RUKHSA mobile
application (the "**App**").

## Not Affiliated with the UAE RTA or Any Government Entity

RUKHSA is an independent, privately developed educational study aid. It is
**not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or in any way officially
connected to the UAE Roads and Transport Authority (RTA), Dubai Police, any
other UAE traffic or licensing authority, or any UAE government entity.** All
trademarks, service marks, and trade names referenced (including "RTA") are
the property of their respective owners and are used solely for descriptive,
nominative reference to identify the subject matter this App helps users
study for. Use of this App does not guarantee success in any official
driving theory examination.

## Summary

RUKHSA is designed to work primarily offline, with your study progress,
question-bank content, and preferences (such as your selected language)
stored **locally on your device**. As of this version, the App does not
require you to create an account, does not require an internet connection to
practice questions, and does not knowingly collect personal information for
the purpose of building a profile about you. Where this changes in a future
version (for example, if we add optional cloud sync, analytics, or account
features), this policy will be updated before that change takes effect, and
where required by law we will seek your consent.

## Information We Collect

### Information stored only on your device
- Your quiz/mock-exam answers, scores, and progress.
- Your selected app language and other in-app preferences.
- Flashcard and mind-map progress/bookmarks.

This data is stored locally using your device's standard app storage and is
not transmitted to us or to any third party by the App itself, unless a
future version explicitly introduces and discloses a sync feature.

### Information that may be collected by app stores and payment processors
If you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is handled by Apple's App
Store or Google Play, not directly by RUKHSA. We do not receive or store
your payment card details. Apple and Google may collect information as
described in their own privacy policies (Apple's App Store and Google Play
Terms of Service and Privacy Policy govern that processing, not this
document).

### Diagnostic and analytics information (if enabled in your app version)
Some versions of the App may use minimal, privacy-respecting crash-reporting
or aggregate usage analytics (for example, to understand which features are
used, or to diagnose crashes) provided by the app stores themselves (such as
Apple App Store Connect analytics or Google Play Console analytics) or a
comparable third-party analytics/crash-reporting provider. Where this is
enabled, it is limited to technical and aggregate usage data (e.g., app
version, device type, crash logs, feature usage counts) and is not used to
identify you personally. If and when the App begins collecting this kind of
data, we will update this section to name the specific provider(s) used and
what is collected.

### Information you provide directly
If you contact us for support or feedback (for example, by email), we will
receive whatever information you choose to include in that message (such as
your email address and the content of your message). We use this only to
respond to you and to improve the App.

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, and the App does not request:
- Your name, government ID, Emirates ID, or driving-licence application
  details.
- Your precise location.
- Access to your contacts, camera, or microphone (the App does not require
  these permissions for its core functionality).
- Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking, unless a future
  ad-supported version explicitly discloses this and provides applicable
  opt-out controls.

## How We Use Information

To the extent any information is collected as described above, we use it
only to:
1. Operate and improve the App's core study features.
2. Diagnose and fix technical issues (crash reports, if enabled).
3. Respond to support requests you send us directly.
4. Process in-app purchases (handled by Apple/Google, not by us directly).

We do not sell your information. We do not share information with third
parties for their own marketing purposes.

## Data Retention

Locally stored data (your progress, preferences, saved flashcards) remains on
your device until you delete the App or clear its data through your device's
settings. We do not maintain a separate server-side copy of this data unless
a future sync feature is introduced and disclosed.

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users who are old enough to be preparing for a UAE
driving licence (16+ depending on licence category and RTA rules in effect at
the time). The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe
a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can
address it.

## Your Choices

- You can use the core study features of the App entirely offline, without
  providing any personal information.
- You can delete all locally stored App data at any time by uninstalling the
  App or clearing its storage via your device settings.
- If a future version introduces optional analytics or cloud sync, we will
  provide a way to opt out where required by applicable law.

## International Users and UAE Residents

The App is designed primarily for individuals preparing for a UAE driving
theory test, who may be located in the UAE or elsewhere. Where applicable UAE
data protection law (including Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the
Protection of Personal Data, as amended) or other local data protection laws
apply to your use of the App, we intend to handle personal information
consistently with those requirements; if you believe we are falling short of
any applicable requirement, please contact us.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example as the App
adds features. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and
for material changes affecting how personal information is handled, we will
provide reasonable notice within the App where feasible.
