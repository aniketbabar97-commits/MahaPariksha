# Syllabus & Exam-Pattern Research (accessed 2026-09-26)

Scope: the objective (MCQ) stages only. Most figures below come from education portals that restate official notifications. Official PDFs on mpsc.gov.in, mahapolice.gov.in and similar sites were not opened directly in this pass, so treat every row as "secondary source" until an official check is done (see section 3). "NM" = negative marking.

## 1. Per-exam patterns

### MPSC Rajyaseva Prelims (Gazetted Civil Services)
| Paper | Qs | Marks | NM | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GS Paper I (history, geography, polity, economy, environment, science, current affairs) | 100 | 200 | 1/4 of question marks | 2 h |
| CSAT Paper II (comprehension, interpersonal/communication skills, reasoning, decision making, basic numeracy, data interpretation, Marathi & English comprehension) | 80 | 200 | 1/4 | 2 h |
CSAT is qualifying at 33%; merit uses Paper I only. Papers are bilingual (Marathi/English). The mains is now descriptive, UPSC-style, and out of scope. Sources: https://www.drishtiias.com/state-pcs/mpsc-exam-pattern, https://www.pw.live/state-psc/exams/mpsc-rajyaseva-exam-analysis-2025-9-november (accessed 2026-09-26).

## 2. Taxonomy changes

### 2a. Subjects to ADD
| id | mr | en | Suggested topics |
|---|---|---|---|
| current_affairs | चालू घडामोडी | Current Affairs | national, maharashtra, international, schemes, sci_tech_news, sports_news, awards_news |
| environment | पर्यावरण व परिस्थितिकी | Environment & Ecology | ecology_basics, biodiversity, climate_change, protected_areas, env_laws |
| csat | नागरी सेवा कल चाचणी (CSAT) | CSAT / Aptitude | comprehension, decision_making, interpersonal, data_interpretation, basic_numeracy |
| evs | परिसर अभ्यास | Environmental Studies (TET) | family_community, food_water, shelter, travel, things_we_make |
| social_studies | सामाजिक शास्त्रे | Social Studies (TET Paper II) | history, geography, civics, pedagogy_sst |
| technical | तांत्रिक विषय | Technical (post-specific) | placeholder for ZP/Arogya technical sections |

### 2b. Topics to ADD to existing subjects
- `english`: `comprehension` (आकलन / Reading Comprehension), `cloze` (रिक्त स्थाने / Cloze Test), `para_jumble` (वाक्यरचना क्रम / Sentence Rearrangement)
- `marathi`: `utara` (उतारा आकलन / Marathi Comprehension)
- `maths`: `data_interpretation` (माहितीचे विश्लेषण / Data Interpretation), `algebra` (बीजगणित / Algebra), `geometry` (भूमिती / Geometry), `trigonometry` (त्रिकोणमिती / Trigonometry), `statistics` (सांख्यिकी / Statistics) for SSC CGL, NDA and CDS
- `reasoning`: `puzzles` (कोडी व बैठक व्यवस्था / Puzzles & Seating), `inequality` (असमानता / Inequality), `nonverbal` (आकृती तर्क / Non-verbal Reasoning), `data_sufficiency` (माहिती पर्याप्तता / Data Sufficiency)
- `science`: `sci_tech` (विज्ञान व तंत्रज्ञान / Science & Technology)
- `economy`: `social_dev` (सामाजिक विकास व योजना / Social Development & Schemes), `mh_economy` (महाराष्ट्राची अर्थव्यवस्था / Maharashtra Economy)
- `geography`: `mh_forests` (महाराष्ट्रातील वने / Forests of Maharashtra) for Van Rakshak
- `polity`: `governance` (प्रशासन व शासनव्यवस्था / Governance)
- `traffic`: confirm it contains `signs`, `rules` and `safe_driving`.

### 2c. Exam→subject mapping corrections
- **mpsc_rajyaseva**: add current_affairs, environment, csat; remove gk; keep maths and reasoning only as CSAT support.
- **mpsc_combined_b / _c**: remove marathi and english (per sources these are tested in the mains, not the prelims); add current_affairs.
- **police_bharti**: add current_affairs; history/geography/polity/science stay as GK sub-areas.
- **talathi**: add current_affairs; remove economy and computer (not separate sections in the sourced pattern).
- **zp_bharti**: add current_affairs, technical; remove computer.
- **van_rakshak**: add environment, current_affairs.
- **arogya_sevak**: add technical, current_affairs.
- **mahatet**: add evs, social_studies; remove history and geography (now under social_studies); keep reasoning and gk for TAIT.
- **ctet**: add marathi (as Language II option), evs, social_studies; remove history and geography.
- **ssc_cgl / chsl / gd / mts**: add current_affairs. For CHSL and MTS, remove computer where it is listed (it appears in the CHSL Tier 2 but not in Tier 1). CGL: computer is also Tier 2 only; move it out of the prelim set.
- **rrb_ntpc**: add current_affairs; computer stays (part of the NTPC GA syllabus).
- **rrb_group_d**: add current_affairs, polity, economy (GA section).
- **ibps_clerk / ibps_po**: the prelims cover only english, maths and reasoning. Move economy, computer and gk to an optional "mains" tag.
- **upsc_prelims**: add current_affairs, environment, csat; replace gk with current_affairs.
- **nda_cds**: add current_affairs; mark maths topics algebra, trigonometry, geometry and statistics.
- **agniveer**: add current_affairs.

## 3. Uncertainties (not confirmed from an official source)
1. No official PDF was opened directly. All numbers are from secondary portals. CTET and UPSC rows are stated from the long-standing official pattern and were not re-fetched.
2. MPSC combined prelims: whether Marathi and English are absent from the Group B and Group C prelims should be verified against the latest MPSC notification (2025 and the 04 Jan 2026 exam).
3. Police Bharti section-wise question split is not confirmed.
4. Van Rakshak negative marking (0.25) and the 120-question count come from a single portal.
5. Agniveer negative marking: sources conflict between none and 0.25.
6. RTO pass mark: sources conflict between 9/15 and 12/15 after the reported 2025 changes. The time limit is also unconfirmed.
7. SSC CHSL sectional timing from 2026 is reported by one portal only.
8. SBI Clerk/PO prelim marks per section were not verified separately from IBPS.
9. The ZP and Arogya patterns are based on the 2023 recruitment cycle; there is no 2025–26 notification confirming them.
10. Paper language (Marathi availability) for SSC and IBPS was not verified per exam.
